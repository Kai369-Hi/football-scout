      How the score works

      Style match

      Cosine similarity between per-90 profiles, standardised within the same position group. Strikers use shot location and header share as well as volume, so a shooting winger is not mistaken for a centre-forward.

      Output level

      A 0-100 percentile score within the role (for example npxG, xA and xG chain for attackers). It stops the scout from suggesting someone with the same style but half the production.

      Value for money

      The market value model estimates a price from performance, age, league and role only. A player valued at a quarter of that estimate scores 100; one at half of it scores 67.

      Known limits

      • Speed and aerial duels are not measured. Free tracking data does not exist, and height is only a rough proxy for heading ability.
      • Defensive counts are not adjusted for possession, so centre-backs at dominant teams can look weaker than they are.
      • The value model underrates how fast prices fall after 32, so older players can look like bargains. Use the age filter.
      • Only each player's main position is known. A right-back who also plays in midfield is compared as a right-back.
      • Fame, injuries, wages and club prestige are invisible to the models. Treat every suggestion as a lead to check, not a verdict.
